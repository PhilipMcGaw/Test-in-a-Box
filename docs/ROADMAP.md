# Roadmap

## v0.1

Hardware configuration, reusable hardware library, mimic panel, Blockly with
parameters and units, mock and PSU-only examples, run/pause/step/stop, progress,
estimated finish time, CSV and Markdown results, and automatic identity capture.

## After v0.1

Better reports and plots, more reusable blocks, richer pass/warn/fail behaviour,
improved recovery and diagnostics, more drivers, and stronger pre-run validation.

## Longer term

Database storage, test versioning, calibration integration, operator workflows,
barcodes, multi-rig dashboards, notifications, andon lights, and production or
end-of-line features.
