# Future Ideas

This file parks valuable ideas that are deliberately outside v0.1.

## Execution

Andon lights, Teams/email/MQTT notifications, adaptive estimated finish time,
restart recovery and multiple concurrent rigs.

## Evaluation

Nominal values and tolerances, upper/lower limits, pass/warn/fail severity,
configurable actions and event detection during ramps.

## Reporting

Plots, PDF reports, databases, statistics and historical comparisons.

## Production

Operator login, barcode scanning, audit trails, recipe control, end-of-line
workflows and MES integration.

## Management

Standards references, approvals, calibration tracking and requirements
traceability.
